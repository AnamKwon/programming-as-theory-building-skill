- `StockAdjustmentRequest(delta=0)` is accepted — `Field(ne=...)` is not a real pydantic constraint, silently ignored.
- `sqlalchemy` and `pydantic-settings` are declared dependencies but unused (raw `sqlite3`; `os.environ` directly).
