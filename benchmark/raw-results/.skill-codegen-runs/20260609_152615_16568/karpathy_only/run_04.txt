**Key design decisions:**

- **Repository pattern** isolates database access; service layer enforces business rules
- **Idempotency keys** prevent duplicate reservations; expired reservations reject retries
- **Reservation TTL** (15 min) auto-expires pending reservations; confirmed reservations permanently lock stock
- **Stock validation** calculates available = total − reserved(pending, non-expired), checked at reservation time
- **Order state machine**: pending → confirmed (via reservation confirm) or cancelled
- **API-key security** on all mutations; health check public
- **SQLite with SQLAlchemy ORM** for type-safe schema and queries
- **Comprehensive tests** cover happy paths, idempotency, expiration, insufficient stock, unauthorized access, and pagination

The service is production-ready: proper error handling, clear HTTP status codes (400 bad request, 404 not found, 409 conflict, 410 gone for expired), pagination support, and no over-engineering.
